Privacy policy

Alfred, a private personal assistant · effective 4 October 2026

Scope

Alfred is personal software used by a single person, its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Alfred does with data from the owner's Google account.

What Google data Alfred accesses

  • Google Calendar events (permission calendar.events): titles, times and descriptions of events in the calendars the owner has listed in Alfred's settings.
  • Google Tasks (permission tasks): titles, due dates, notes and status of the owner's tasks.

Alfred does not request access to mail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • To show, create, move, rename and delete the owner's events and tasks when the owner asks.
  • To include today's events and open tasks in the owner's morning summary.

Alfred never adds other people to events and sends event changes without notifications to anyone.

Where the data goes

  • The Google access key is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Event and task details are processed on the owner's computer by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) that carries out the owner's requests, and the results are delivered to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Alfred's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored key from the computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support address shown on Alfred's Google sign-in screen.
