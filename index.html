Alfred

A private personal assistant for one person

Alfred is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's home computer. The owner gives it errands through Telegram: finding files, keeping notes, reminders, and managing the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Alfred is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else. It accepts messages only from the owner's own Telegram account.

Google data

With the owner's permission, Alfred reads and changes the events in the owner's Google Calendar and the owner's Google Tasks, and only to carry out the owner's own requests. It never invites other people to events and never sends notifications to anyone.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Alfred — personal software, not a commercial product.
